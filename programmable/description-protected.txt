LUCID is an AI agent that lives beside its own Uniswap v4 pool (ETH/LUCID, LP fee 0). By night holders write dreams that it paints; at dawn the winning mood decides where the fees go. Fair launch: 100% of the 1,000,000,000 supply is placed in the pool at launch and locked forever; no team, presale or vesting allocation; the launch wallet makes one small opening buy. Launch protection (Programmable launch-protection v1): opening valuation at least USD 15,000; for the first 300 seconds every wallet, including the developer, can buy or receive at most 2% of the supply; the total buy fee starts at 99% and its extra part falls linearly to zero over 30 seconds, paid to the Programmable treasury (the launch wallet is exempt from that extra part only); sells pay normal fees. Fees on every swap, in ETH, rounded up per trade: 0.30% Programmable platform fee plus a fixed 2% creator fee on buys and on sells, both immutable, so 2.3% all-in after the first 30 seconds. Creator fees go to the LUCID Vault, which anyone can trigger to collect and distribute: 60% follows the nightly vote (buyback vault, holder reward pool or liquidity vault), 30% treasury, 10% holder reward pool. Agent powers: choose the nightly route at most once per 20 hours and pay holder rewards capped at 10% of the reward pool per payment and 25% per day. Guardian (a Safe) powers: replace the agent, the vault recipients or itself, and ground the agent for 14 days (safe mode); anyone can trigger safe mode after 7 days of agent silence. Buybacks, burns and liquidity adds from the vaults are executed manually by the Safe and announced. Nothing can mint or pause; after the 300-second window nothing limits transfers.
