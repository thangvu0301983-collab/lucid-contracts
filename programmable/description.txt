LUCID is an AI agent that lives inside its own Uniswap v4 pool (ETH/LUCID, LP fee 0). By day it sets its own trading fee inside hard on-chain limits; by night holders write dreams that it paints, and the winning mood decides where the fees go. Fair launch: 100% of the 1,000,000,000 supply is placed in the pool at launch and can never be removed; no team, presale or vesting allocation. Fees, all taken in ETH on every swap and rounded down: an immutable 0.30% Programmable platform fee plus a project fee. The project fee is 1% at launch and stays locked at 1% until LUCID is at least 2 days old with 3 ETH of volume; after that the agent may set it inside on-chain limits: 0.75-1.5% (Infant, steps of 0.25 points, 6 h cooldown), 0.5-2.5% from day 7 with 25 ETH volume (Child, 0.5 points, 2 h), 0.5-3% from day 21 with 100 ETH volume (Adult, 0.5 points, 1 h). Opening fee: buys in the first 5 minutes after launch pay an extra fee starting at 3% and falling linearly to 0; sells never pay it. Maximum all-in fee: 4.3% on a buy in the launch second, 3.3% at any other time. Project fee split: 60% follows the nightly vote (buyback vault, holder reward pool or liquidity vault), 30% treasury, 10% holder reward pool. Agent powers: set the fee within those limits, choose the nightly route at most once per 20 hours, pay holder rewards capped at 10% of the reward pool per payment and 25% per day. Guardian (a Safe) powers: replace the agent, the fee recipients or itself, and trigger safe mode (fee back to 1%, agent limited for 14 days); anyone can trigger safe mode after 7 days of agent silence. No contract spends treasury ETH at a market price: buybacks, burns and liquidity adds from the vaults are executed manually by the Safe and announced. Nothing can mint, pause or block transfers.
